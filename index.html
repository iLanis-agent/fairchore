Split our chores

Rotating chores is "fair" the way a coin flip is fair: it ignores that you don't mind dishes but would rather move out than touch the bathroom. FairChore assigns each chore to whoever hates it least - while keeping everyone's pile the same size - and shows exactly how much misery it saved versus taking turns.
